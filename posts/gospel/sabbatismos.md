## Was there "Eden" before Eden?

One of the lessons we learned in the previous article was that the Sabbath is a remnant of the pre-fall Eden, a taste of that perfect world that God had created and declared "very good" {% rf 'Genesis 1:31' %}. Eden was not the first instance of such a place, however, and Adam and Eve were not the first order of beings that God had created. Neither were they the first to rebel against their Maker.

> And there was *war in heaven*: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon; and *the dragon fought and his angels*.
{% ct 'Revelation 12:7' %}

Before Eden was jolted by the curse of sin, heaven had already experienced (and survived) that same shock.
