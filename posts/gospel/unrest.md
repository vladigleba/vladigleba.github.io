## Was there “Eden” before Eden?

One of the lessons we learned in the previous article was that the Sabbath is a remnant of Eden before the fall, a taste of that perfect world that God had created and declared “very good” {% rf 'Genesis 1:31' %}. Eden was not the first instance of such a place, however, and Adam and Eve were not the first order of beings that God had created. Neither were they the first to rebel against their Maker.

> And there was *war in heaven*: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon; and *the dragon fought and his angels*.
{% ct 'Revelation 12:7' %}

Before the curse of sin had wrecked Eden, it had already jolted heaven, piercing the heart of God and separating His angels into two opposite and irreconcilable groups: the good and the evil. Of those who chose to join the evil side, it is inconceivable to assume that they actively desired to be evil initially. It is more likely that they were deceived through clever insinuations that sounded reasonable and attractive. Someone must have been promising them an even better version of heaven than the one created by God.

## Who was their deceiver?

> 12 How art thou *fallen from heaven, O Lucifer*, son of the morning! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst weaken the nations!
{% ct 'Isaiah 14:12' %}

Lucifer (meaning “light-bearer”) was Satan’s name before his fall from heaven, for Jesus clearly identifies this fallen-from-heaven being: “I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven” {% rf 'Luke 10:18' %}. Lucifer was not merely an angel though, but a covering cherub. Ezekiel describes him under the symbolism of the king of Tyre:

> Thou hast been *in Eden the garden of God*; … Thou art *the anointed cherub that covereth; and I have set thee so*: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire.
{% rf 'Ezekiel 28:14' %}

## What is a covering cherub?

When God spoke to Moses on top of Mount Sinai, He instructed him to build a sanctuary, so He could dwell among them {% rf 'Exodus 25:8' %}. The sanctuary consisted of two compartments: the Holy Place and the Most Holy Place, and it was in the latter that the presence of God dwelt, above the Ark of the Covenant’s mercy seat, between the two cherubim. 

> And there I will meet with thee, and *I will commune with thee from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubims which are upon the ark of the testimony*, of all things which I will give thee in commandment unto the children of Israel.
{% ct 'Exodus 25:22' %}

As a covering cherub appointed by God Himself, Lucifer had the privilege to abide in God’s presence, a special honor that set him apart from the rest of the angels and gave him influence over them.

## What happened to him?

Lucifer was perfect before God until iniquity was found in Him:

> Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, *till iniquity was found in thee*. By the multitude of thy merchandise they have filled the midst of thee with violence, and *thou hast sinned* …
{% ct 'Ezekiel 28:15-16' %}

The fact that Lucifer sinned implies that there was a law for him (and other angels) to sin against {% rf '1 John 3:4' %}. This law God had established in order to secure order and peace for all the inhabitants of His kingdom:

> *Great peace have they which love thy law*: and nothing shall offend them.
{% ct 'Psalm 119:165' %}

> O that thou hadst *hearkened to my commandments! then had thy peace been as a river*, and thy righteousness as the waves of the sea.
{% ct 'Isaiah 48:18' %}

Rebellion against God's law, therefore, could only bring unrest:

> But the wicked are like the troubled sea, when it *cannot rest*, whose waters cast up mire and dirt. *There is no peace, saith my God, to the wicked*.
{% ct 'Isaiah 47:20-21' %}

## What is the purpose of the gospel?

> Let us therefore fear, lest, *a promise being left us of entering into his rest*, any of you should seem to come short of it. ... *There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God*. For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.
{% ct 'Hebrews 4:1, 9-10' %}

Prior to Lucifer's rebellion, God's kingdom was in a state of rest--*sabbatismos*.
